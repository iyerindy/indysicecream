About

Hi! I am Indy.

I love ice cream… that is my main credential. I also hate ice cream with no toppings.

That's me up there, a few decades into my research. Indy's is my tiny ice cream company: one flavor a month, made by hand in Santa Monica in one small batch, never repeated. It'll grow someday. For now it's ten pints and a lot of taste testing.
